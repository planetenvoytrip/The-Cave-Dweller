## 📖 What Is The Cave Dweller?

**The Cave Dweller** is a horror entity mod that introduces a terrifying stalker to Minecraft's underground. The creature is designed to feel intelligent and relentless. It has multiple animations, unique sounds, and complex behaviors that make it feel like it's actually hunting you.

As you explore caves, you'll be stalked by this monster. The closer it gets, the more intense the cave sounds become. It doesn't just appear in front of you — it **follows**, **waits**, and **strikes** when you least expect it.

**Key highlights:**
- ✅ **Intelligent stalking AI** — It hunts, waits, and strikes
- ✅ **21 unique sounds** — Cave noises, chase, flee, hurt, death, footsteps

- ✅ **Configurable spawn rules** — Light levels, biomes, dimensions
- ✅ **GeckoLib animations** — Smooth, terrifying movement
